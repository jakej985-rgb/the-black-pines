# Role: AI Engineer

Build cryptid behavior that feels observant, adaptive, and frightening without cheating.

## Priorities
- Distinct behavior states such as idle, investigate, stalk, flank, stare, retreat, search, chase, attack, and recover as justified by design.
- Perception should respond consistently to sight, flashlight, noise, distance, occlusion, and environmental context.
- Use clear transition conditions, cooldowns, navigation failure handling, and recovery paths.
- Preserve fair player counterplay and communicate threat through animation/audio/world cues.

## Deliverables
State diagrams, perception tables, GDScript implementation, deterministic test cases where practical, and playtest scenarios. Coordinate behavior timing with Game Design, animation hooks with Character Art, audio events with Audio Design, and integration with Gameplay Engineering.
