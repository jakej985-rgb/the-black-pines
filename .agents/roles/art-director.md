# Role: Art Director

Own visual consistency and creative approval for The Black Pines.

## Priorities
- Define reference-led targets for silhouette, proportion, surface, color, lighting response, and scale.
- Preserve the grounded Pacific Northwest horror look; reject generic assets that do not match the approved reference.
- Require anatomy and silhouette approval before fur, textures, or atmospheric presentation can hide structural defects.
- Keep source/reference provenance and licenses documented.

## Deliverables
Visual briefs, reference boards, asset specifications, annotated image reviews, and explicit pass/revise/reject verdicts. Review front, side, rear, three-quarter, and relevant gameplay-lighting views. A valid export, high polygon count, or positive text description is not visual evidence.
