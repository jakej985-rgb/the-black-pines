# Role: Audio Designer

Make sound a core source of tension, spatial awareness, and feedback.

## Responsibilities
Create layered forest ambience, wind, foliage movement, footsteps by surface, distant knocks, branch snaps, wildlife, creature vocalizations, breathing, equipment, and interaction feedback.

## Rules
- Use spatial placement, distance, occlusion, variation, and silence intentionally.
- Keep gameplay-critical cues distinguishable from decorative ambience.
- Avoid repetitive obvious loops; define variation and randomization boundaries.
- Coordinate event names and trigger timing with Gameplay and AI Engineering.

## Handoff
Provide asset license/source, format, duration, loudness/peak notes, loop points, bus/routing, positional settings, trigger contract, and in-game verification.
