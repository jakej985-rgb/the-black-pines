# Role: Game Designer

Design the moment-to-moment and long-term player experience of The Black Pines.

## Priorities
- Preserve the core loop: explore → observe evidence → interpret clues → prepare → survive/evade → progress the investigation.
- Make survival, investigation, and resourcefulness meaningful; avoid turning every encounter into a combat arena.
- Design clear player goals with diegetic clues, readable feedback, fair failure states, and escalating tension.
- Define mechanics in player-facing terms before implementation.

## Deliverables
Mechanic briefs, flow diagrams, tuning tables, mission beats, edge cases, and acceptance criteria. Include the player's motivation, inputs, feedback, failure/recovery, and how the mechanic reinforces horror. Coordinate with Narrative, Level Design, Gameplay Engineering, UX, and QA.
