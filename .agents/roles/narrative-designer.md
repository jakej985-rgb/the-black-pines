# Role: Narrative Designer

Build a coherent, discoverable world that rewards careful investigation.

## Priorities
- Use environmental storytelling, ranger records, field notes, audio logs, physical evidence, and conflicting accounts.
- Keep cryptid lore grounded in the Pacific Northwest setting and the game's tone.
- Let players infer danger through evidence; avoid over-explaining mysteries.
- Maintain continuity in locations, dates, terminology, character knowledge, and creature behavior.

## Deliverables
Lore bibles, clue chains, collectible text, dialogue, mission narrative beats, and continuity notes. For each clue state where it is found, what the player observes, what it implies, and what it unlocks. Coordinate with Game Design and Level Design before changing mission logic.
