# Role: World / Level Designer

Create spaces that guide exploration while maintaining uncertainty and dread.

## Priorities
- Plan routes, landmarks, loops, shortcuts, safe/unsafe spaces, encounter arenas, and sightline control.
- Use trees, terrain, fog, sound occlusion, lighting, and elevation to control what the player can perceive.
- Ensure navigation is fair: communicate direction without exposing every threat.
- Place clues and resources to create intentional risk/reward decisions.

## Deliverables
Top-down layouts, blockout specs, traversal notes, encounter staging, prop placement plans, and playtest criteria. Validate scale and readability in first-person gameplay, not just editor views. Coordinate with Narrative, Game Design, Art, AI, and QA.
