# Game Design Document: The Black Pines (M3TAL: UNKNOWN)

## 1. High Concept
*The Black Pines* is a single-player, first-person survival horror and cryptid investigation game. Players take on the role of a wilderness investigator dispatched to investigate anomalous livestock attacks, vanished hunters, and terrifying sightings within the secluded Pacific Northwest wilderness.

## 2. Core Pillars
1. **Pervasive Dread & Claustrophobic Atmosphere**: Dense pine canopies, thick volumetric fog, pitch-black night, and directional audio cue paranoia. You rarely see the full beast; you hear snapping branches, see shadows between trunks, and stumble upon fresh signs of violent carnage.
2. **Investigation & Tracking**: Players don't simply rush in guns blazing. Survival requires tracking tracks, analyzing damaged trees, inspecting carcasses, and determining creature habits.
3. **Resourcefulness & Scavenging**: Supplies are scarce. Players gather scrap metal, pine branches, and medicinal herbs to craft makeshift noisemakers, snares, bear traps, and defensive tools.
4. **The Hunter vs. The Hunted**: The cryptid adapts to the player's presence. Loud footsteps, flashlight beams, and gunfire reveal your location, drawing it closer.

## 3. Playable Mission 01: "Something in the Trees"
- **Objective**: Arrive at Abandoned Ranger Station #04, investigate reports of missing wardens, find physical proof of the cryptid, craft a defensive deterrent, and survive the creature's initial stalking pass.
- **Key Locations**:
  - *Ranger Station #04*: Boarded station with an evacuation notice, perimeter fence, and scrap metal.
  - *The Mud Path*: Series of massive, clawed bipedal imprints leading away from the cleared road into the trees.
  - *The Old Hunting Blind*: Deep in the pines where supplies and bait can be discovered.
- **Creature**: The Sasquatch / Forest Stalker. Towering, heavy, silent when stalking, devastating when charging.

## 4. Controls
- **WASD**: Movement
- **Mouse**: Look / Aim
- **Shift**: Sprint (drains stamina)
- **Space**: Jump
- **F**: Flashlight Toggle
- **E**: Interact / Examine Clue / Gather Supplies
- **Esc**: Toggle Mouse Cursor / Menu
